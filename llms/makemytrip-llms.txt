# MakeMyTrip Overview > MakeMyTrip is a leading online travel agency providing booking services for
flights, hotels, trains, bus, cabs, visa,  holiday packages, forex and travel insurance

## Key Features
- **Flight Bookings**: Search and book domestic and international flights.
- **Hotel Bookings**: Book hotels across various destinations worldwide.
- **Holiday Packages**: Explore pre-designed holiday packages for popular destinations.
- **Business Travel Solutions**: Introducing myBiz for corporate travel management.
-**Trip Ideas**: Explore Places, Things to Do, know Best Time to Visit  before planing your trip.
-**Train Bookings**: Search and book train tickets across India.
-**Bus Bookings**: Book intercity bus tickets for various routes.
-**Cab Services**: Book cabs for local and outstation travel.
-**Homestays and Villas**: Alternative accommodation options for a homely experience.
-**Visa Services**: Assistance with visa applications for international travel.
-**Gift Cards**: Purchase travel gift cards for friends and family.
-**Travel Insurance**: Add-on insurance options for trip protection.
